# Hypothesis Testing Results

## Hypothesis 1 — Role vs Tool Adoption

**H0:** There is no significant relationship between tool adoption and role in HR.

**Result:** χ² = 3.4597, p = 0.9023.

**Decision:** Fail to reject H0 at the 5% significance level.

## Hypothesis 2 — Experience vs Tool Impact Rating

**H0:** There is no significant relationship between tool impact and experience.

**Result:** χ² = 13.7184, p = 0.3190.

**Decision:** Fail to reject H0 at the 5% significance level.

## Hypothesis 3 — Company Size vs Training Needs

**H0:** There is no significant relationship between training needs and company size.

**Result:** χ² = 17.7952, p = 0.0228.

**Decision:** Reject H0 at the 5% significance level.
