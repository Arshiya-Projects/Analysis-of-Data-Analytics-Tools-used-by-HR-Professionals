## Objective

To examine the adoption and usage of data analytics tools among HR professionals and study relationships involving role, experience, company size, perceived tool impact, and training requirements.

## Data Collection

The report states that primary data was collected through a structured Google Form and that 170 responses were received.

## Data Analysis

The report describes data cleaning and preparation in Python, followed by descriptive visualization and inferential analysis using Chi-square tests of independence.

## Visualizations

The report documents visualizations covering:

- Tool adoption by role
- Tool impact by experience
- Training needs by company size
- Total respondents
- Experience distribution
- Analytics-tool usage
- Frequently used analytics tools
- Industry distribution
- Challenges faced by HR teams
- Tableau dashboard
